1. The golden rule

Use Basic to mark the market. Use Advanced only to control extrapolation. A trade inside the quoted strike range is evidence about a node or a group of nodes—not a reason to reshape an unquoted tail.

Basic · 11-node smile editor Changes the actual operational smile between the lowest and highest official strikes. This is the normal intraday tool.
Advanced · Wing tail model Changes only the unquoted low- and high-strike tails and their joins to the core. This is an expert/reviewer tool.
Do not fit every print. Check time, size, bid/ask, strategy context, option type, contract month, forward and stale-market risk. A single small trade can be a bad surface mark even when its implied volatility is mathematically valid.

ICE traded options are displayed as an independent diagnostic overlay from gas_options_activity. They are not automatic calibration targets. The official 11-node smile remains the starting surface; the trader decides whether a new trade contains enough information to change the session Final.

2. How the operational model works

Each expiry is built in total variance versus log-moneyness. The page first converts the 11 governed call-delta observations into strikes and then into the coordinates below.

T = DTE / 365time to expiry in years
x = ln(K / F)log-moneyness
w(x) = T × σ(x)²total variance

Conceptual diagram, not a market curve. The 11 dots are reproduced exactly by the PCHIP core.

The four layers

  1. Governed inputs. One expiry must contain the complete 11-node call-delta grid, one positive forward, positive DTE and homogeneous provenance. The Basis is either Observed or Extrapolated.
  2. Authoritative PCHIP core. A shape-preserving cubic interpolation is built through all 11 total-variance nodes. It is used exactly between the minimum and maximum official strikes.
  3. Wing-v2 tail fit. Wing-v2 is fitted to 201 samples of the PCHIP core. This gives a smooth, economically bounded base from which to extrapolate; it does not replace the core.
  4. C1 Hermite joins. Outside each core boundary, a cubic-Hermite blend connects PCHIP value and slope to Wing value and slope. Automatic calibration tries widths 0.10, 0.15, 0.20 and 0.30 in log-moneyness until the full surface passes.
Two different meanings of “tail”. An Extrapolated Basis is a later expiry whose 11 input nodes come from the governed term-structure template. A Wing tail is the unquoted strike region outside the 11 nodes of any single expiry. They are not the same thing.

What Wing-v2 describes

Inside its central regions, the Wing base is:

σ(x<0) = vr + sr·x + pc·x²lower-strike / put side
σ(x≥0) = vr + sr·x + cc·x²higher-strike / call side
0 ≤ βput, βcall < 2Lee-compatible far-tail slopes

The raw Wing model has six regions: deep put tail, put smoothing, central put, central call, call smoothing and deep call tail. Wing-v2 makes the deep tails C1-continuous and asymptotically linear in total variance. The database names put_wing_power and call_wing_power are legacy names; on the page they appear as PTVS and CTVS, and in Wing-v2 they are total-variance slopes—not power exponents.

3. A disciplined intraday workflow

  1. Lock the context. Confirm COB date, TTF expiry, forward, DTE and Basis. Do not compare a live trade with a stale-forward surface as if the difference were pure volatility.
  2. Establish a valid starting surface. Use Calibrate for one expiry or Calibrate All Expiries for the governed set. Confirm Arb = Pass before making a discretionary edit.
  3. Read the trade, not only the marker. Check timestamp, volume, price quality, strategy/leg context and whether the option belongs to the displayed expiry. Recompute the implied vol using the same forward and convention.
  4. Choose the right control. If the evidence is within the 11-node strike range, change Basic. If the issue exists only beyond the outer nodes, review Advanced.
  5. Make the smallest coherent change. Adjust one node only when the evidence is truly local. Otherwise move adjacent nodes to express level, skew or curvature without creating a saw-tooth smile.
  6. Let the gate decide feasibility. Every Basic or Advanced Final rebuild is checked across the full hybrid. If rejected, reduce or broaden the change; do not defeat the gate.
  7. Review in three coordinates. Check Delta for trader intuition, log-moneyness for model geometry and moneyness/strike for economic placement. Inspect neighboring expiries as a separate calendar sanity check.
  8. Export before handover. The TTF hybrid is session-only and is not saved to the ordinary Wing parameter store. Export is the handover/audit artifact until a governed publication path exists.
Forward consistency is non-negotiable. Delta, strike and implied volatility move together. If the intraday trade was interpreted with a different forward from the page, apparent skew changes can be coordinate changes rather than new volatility information.

4. Using the Basic 11-node smile editor

The Basic table is the trader’s mark sheet. Settlement IV (%) is the immutable reference loaded for the selected COB; Final IV (%) is the editable in-session mark.

ColumnMeaningHow to use it
Call DeltaThe governed raw call delta, from 0.01 to 0.99.Use it to identify the quoted node. A lower call delta is a higher-strike call-wing node; a higher call delta is a lower-strike put-wing node.
StrikeThe strike implied by the node’s delta, IV, DTE and forward under the governed convention.Match trade economics here. It is read-only because the grid is governed by delta.

Delta chart convention. The Basic table shows raw Call Delta. The chart’s Delta view uses a trader-style put-to-ATM-to-call coordinate: roughly 0.10 = 10Δ put, 0.50 = ATM and 0.90 = 10Δ call. Therefore a 0.10 raw Call Delta node appears near 0.90 on that chart.

What happens after an edit

  1. The entered Final IV is converted back to decimal IV and total variance.
  2. The PCHIP core is rebuilt through all 11 Final nodes.
  3. The Wing tail is refitted from the current row as its starting geometry.
  4. The complete PCHIP/blend/Wing hybrid is validated.
  5. If valid, the session Final and chart update. If invalid, the edit is blocked and the accepted rows are restored.

The PCHIP core passes exactly through the edited nodes. This is useful because the desk mark is preserved; it is also dangerous because the model does not average away a poor mark. PCHIP is shape-preserving, not a noise filter and not an automatic arbitrage projection.

Automatic versus manual freedom. Automatic TTF fitting moves vr, sr, pc, cc, dc, uc, PTVS and CTVS. It carries dsm, usm, VCR, SCR and SSR from the starting row. The table permits manual edits to more fields than a normal trader should need.

Calibrate, Copy Candidate and Reset

  • Calibrate fits a candidate for the selected expiry and opens the Current / Candidate / Final comparison.
  • Copy Candidate to Final copies the fitted tail parameters into the in-session Final, subject to the complete hybrid validation.
  • Reset Final to Current abandons the proposed tail change and restores the row that was Current when the comparison opened.
  • Manual Final edits in the comparison table rebuild and validate the complete hybrid immediately.
  • Database persistence is blocked. The TTF Save Final handler refuses hybrid publication and Save All is disabled. Copying to Final is session state, not a database write.

What Calibrate All Expiries does

Observed expiries are fitted from their own editable rows. Extrapolated expiries are processed chronologically: the first one starts from the latest accepted earlier fit, and each later one starts from the last successful result. The extrapolated fit uses three deterministic starts and retries with nine only when the first run produces no accepted candidate. An isolated failure leaves that row unchanged and the chain continues from the last accepted seed. “Skip good fits” applies only to Observed rows.

6. Reading diagnostics and validation gates

DisplayWhat it really measuresWhat it does not prove
Core TV RMSETotal-variance error of the operational PCHIP core against its own 11 nodes. For an unchanged valid core it is numerically zero by construction.It does not prove the input nodes are tradable, current or economically correct.
Wing Tail TV RMSETotal-variance RMSE of the internal Wing approximation against 201 PCHIP samples over the core range.It is not the operational core fit—the PCHIP remains authoritative there—and it is not directly a quoted-vol error.
IV DiagnosticThe Wing approximation error translated into implied-volatility terms.It is a secondary diagnostic, not the primary optimization target or a market-quality score.
Arb = PassThe full hybrid passed finite positive variance, Lee-compatible slopes, decreasing and convex Black call prices, and the g ≥ 0.006 butterfly margin on 4,001 points.It does not check calendar arbitrage between expiries, liquidity, bid/ask or whether a discretionary mark is defensible.
Observed / Extrapolated BasisProvenance of the 11 input nodes for that expiry.It does not describe whether a plotted strike is inside or outside the core; that is the core/blend/tail classification.

Validation covers at least min(-0.50, xmin−0.30) to max(0.50, xmax+0.30) in log-moneyness. A rejected edit is not necessarily “wrong” as a market view; it means the proposed nodes and chosen tail geometry cannot produce an accepted static cross-strike surface under the current gate.

Interpret zero correctly. Core TV RMSE of zero means “the PCHIP reproduces the entered nodes exactly.” It does not mean “the surface has zero uncertainty.”

7. Common intraday situations

A liquid ATM strip reprices higher

Action: raise the 40D, 50D and 60D Final IV nodes coherently; include 30D/70D if the repricing is broad.

Avoid: raising vr in Advanced. That changes only the Wing approximation/tails, not the operational PCHIP core.

Calls richen versus puts

Action: adjust the relevant low raw-call-delta nodes—typically 10D, 20D and 30D call—while preserving a smooth progression into ATM.

Check: remember these appear on the right-hand side of the chart’s trader Delta axis.

One small 1D call trades at an extreme IV

Action: treat it as diagnostic until size, bid/ask, time and inversion inputs are verified. Consider no change or a small 1D/10D paired move.

Avoid: forcing CTVS to match a noisy print; the print is still inside the core if it is one of the governed nodes.

The core is right but the far call tail turns oddly

Action: inspect Right Blend and CTVS. A wider blend can soften the join; CTVS controls asymptotic total-variance growth.

Check: review log-moneyness and keep the complete arbitrage gate. Do not alter Basic nodes merely to repair an unquoted tail.

A trade is between two official nodes

Action: infer the smallest adjacent-node changes that make the PCHIP pass reasonably through the trade while respecting its bid/ask.

Avoid: inserting a twelfth node ad hoc; the governed editor requires the complete fixed 11-node grid.

A later extrapolated expiry trades

Action: verify contract and forward, then use the trade as evidence to replace or reshape its Extrapolated Basis Final nodes in session.

Keep visible: provenance remains Extrapolated until a governed observed-source process changes it.

8. Drawbacks and where the implementation is overcomplicated

  1. Too many raw controls for a tail-only job. The Advanced table exposes 13 Wing parameters plus two blend widths. Because the PCHIP core is authoritative, most traders need only tail slope, tail onset and blend width on each side.
  2. Several parameters are weakly identified. Different combinations of vr/sr/pc/cc/dc/uc can approximate the same core yet produce different unquoted tails. A low tail-fit RMSE does not make the parameters unique.
  3. There are two smoothing systems. dsm/usm smooth internal Wing regions; Left/Right Blend joins PCHIP to Wing. Both are mathematically legitimate, but exposing both encourages accidental double-tuning.
  4. VCR, SCR and SSR add no same-snapshot value here. They matter only when the model is evaluated with a current forward different from a reference forward. The current static TTF page defaults the reference to the same forward, neutralizing them.
  5. Exact interpolation preserves noise. PCHIP exactly respects all 11 edits. This is transparent and controllable, but one poor node becomes part of the operational core instead of being smoothed away.
  6. Static arbitrage is not calendar arbitrage. Each expiry is checked across strike. The page does not enforce total-variance ordering, forward consistency or smoothness across maturities.
  7. Delta grids move with market inputs. A node is governed by delta, while traders often discuss a fixed strike. When the forward moves, comparing the same delta can compare different economic strikes.
  8. Far tails have limited observability. Wing-v2 gives disciplined extrapolation and Lee-compatible slopes, but beyond the quoted strikes the answer remains a model judgment.
  9. Session-only state limits auditability. The hybrid is deliberately blocked from ordinary database saving. A refresh/process restart or handover should never be assumed to preserve an unexported surface.
Recommended simplification. Keep the complete Wing-v2 engine underneath, but present a trader Advanced view with only six governed controls: left/right tail onset, left/right total-variance slope, and left/right blend width. Freeze dsm/usm, VCR, SCR and SSR; keep vr/sr/pc/cc optimizer-managed. The full raw table can remain in an expert/audit view.

Governance principles

  • Preserve Settlement and source provenance; only Final is discretionary.
  • Never turn an ICE diagnostic marker into an automatic target without trade-quality controls.
  • Never use Advanced to disguise an incorrect Basic node.
  • Never accept “Arb Pass” as sufficient evidence that a surface is a fair market mark.
  • Record why a node moved, which trade/quote supported it, the forward used and who reviewed it.

10. Pre-export and handover checklist

  • Correct COB, expiry, forward and DTE
  • Observed/Extrapolated Basis understood
  • Trade time, volume and bid/ask checked
  • Trade implied vol uses the page forward/convention
  • Basic changes are coherent across adjacent nodes
  • No Advanced change used to repair a core mark
  • Core TV RMSE interpreted as an exact-fit metric
  • Wing Tail TV RMSE treated as diagnostic only
  • Arb = Pass on the final hybrid
  • Delta, moneyness and log-moneyness views reviewed
  • Neighboring expiries checked manually
  • ICE markers treated as diagnostic evidence
  • Export created before closing/handover
  • No assumption of database persistence
Desk summary: change the 11 nodes when your view of the quoted smile changes; change Wing tail controls only when your view of unquoted extrapolation changes. Keep the edit small, preserve provenance, and let the full arbitrage gate reject impossible geometry.
