Fit and review the complete candidate

Brent settlement: fit once, sample afterward

  1. Check the snapshot UUID, COB and settlement status. Calibration uses eligible out-of-the-money puts below the forward and calls at or above it, with extra weight around the smile body. Pending settlement open interest uses equal liquidity weights and is disclosed in the status text.
  2. Run Calibrate selected snapshot. Each accepted observed expiry has one constrained SVI fit, with a smooth tapered wing extension beyond observed strike support. Missing maturities between accepted fits interpolate total variance; maturities outside them carry the nearest fitted volatility shape with time scaling. Treat these maturities as model-derived coverage.
  3. Inspect fitted versus target expiry counts, minimum implied density and representative front, body and long-end contracts. The preview is a sample of the continuous model. The shared 11 deltas and the dense publication points are sampled after fitting; they do not drive a second PCHIP/Wing fit or a later grid projection.
  4. Review differences at matching strikes, forwards and option expiries. A smooth preview or full target count alone does not establish a good quote fit; check the fit-error gates and observed/model-derived provenance.
Policy: brent_single_svi_actual_strikes_v1. The current workflow replaces the earlier SVI → projected delta grid → hybrid-smile sequence.

Brent intraday: update only supported expiries

  1. Select the intended INTRADAY snapshot and inspect both its as-of time and the preceding settlement anchor. Rolling reduces time to expiry while preserving the accepted settlement IV shape before quote updates.
  2. Use the same calibration button. Quotes must have resolved executable IV, the supported American pricing model, matching governed expiries, acceptable option spreads and synchronized option/underlying capture times.
  3. Check the number of expiries updated by executable quotes. Each replacement must pass the shape and quote-error gates and improve on the settlement model against those quotes. Sparse, stale or rejected expiries remain on the settlement model.
  4. If the status says no expiry improved the accepted settlement fit, there is no new candidate to publish. Obtain a suitable new snapshot and review it again. Before publication, the system rebuilds from the pinned database sources and checks the reviewed candidate fingerprint and active base revision.
The fitted forward stays attached to an accepted intraday smile. An excluded quote must not move its strike coordinates afterward.

HH: preserve the observed fit and seasonality

  1. Read the Input and provenance ledger. HH independently inverts LNE settlement premiums using premium-upfront Black-76, paired NG forwards, captured rates and governed option expiries. ON, ICE PHE, synthetic and alternate-COB observations cannot replace this source. An ON view without a complete same-COB LNE settlement cannot calibrate. Financing rates retain their original batch capture timestamps: they need not equal the completed snapshot timestamp. The captured UTC date must be from settlement COB through four days afterward, and capture cannot follow snapshot completion beyond its sub-second clock precision. Missing, stale, future or inconsistent rates block the candidate; no rate or timestamp is restamped.
  2. Run Calibrate complete HH surface. Liquid months fit SVI to actual eligible strikes, with stronger body weights. The seasonal estimate requires at least 24 accepted liquid months and 12 matched year-on-year delivery-month pairs.
  3. Review thin and distant months separately. Each starts from the accepted same delivery month one year earlier, adjusted by a bounded annual log-ATM trend. Sparse quotes constrain level and skew; five or more distinct strikes can support a fuller SVI fit with a seasonal prior. With no quotes, the month is model-only and its RMSE is shown as No quotes.
  4. Inspect Expiry selection and diagnostics: eligible quotes, RMSE, arbitrage status and source class. observed_fit identifies liquid fits; sparse_quote_constrained covers the seasonal branch, so check quote count as well to identify model-only months. Use Candidate versus active HH surface and Export to review the entire batch.
  5. Both ON and LNE show the same exact-COB HH publication, labelled HH surface · LNE calibrated for the new policy. Older policy revisions are labelled Legacy HH surface. If no publication exists for that date, the view reports it rather than borrowing another date.
Policy: hh_lne_single_svi_seasonal_quotes_v1. The final SVI model supplies both the 11-delta and dense samples. The old calendar/butterfly grid projection and second hybrid fit are removed from this candidate path. Density is still checked; cross-month calendar monotonicity is inapplicable here because the delivery-month NG futures are different underlyings.

TTF and JKM: preserve nodes, validate tails

  1. Use Reload, then check exact-COB forwards, positive time to the verified option expiry and all 11 undiscounted call-delta nodes: 1%, 10%, 20%, 30%, 40%, 50%, 60%, 70%, 80%, 90% and 99%. Each expiry must have one accepted source and one provenance class.
  2. Keep observed official nodes distinct from extrapolated template nodes. Approved extrapolated smiles are eligible calibration targets, but their successful fit does not turn them into market observations. Missing or mixed provenance blocks calibration.
  3. Calibrate reviews the selected expiry. The authoritative core preserves its nodes using PCHIP in total variance versus log-moneyness. For TTF, a failing core triggers a fixed-anchor convex call-price interpolant, provided the original node prices satisfy the required price bounds, decreasing-price and convexity conditions. This changes interpolation between anchors, never the settlement nodes. Passing PCHIP cores retain their existing values. Wing-v2 tails join outside the selected core through smooth transitions. Review Core TV RMSE, Wing tail TV RMSE, IV RMSE and the complete hybrid’s arbitrage result separately. A zero core error only confirms node reproduction. Review the selected core interpolation, original PCHIP density and repaired-core density in the expiry diagnostics; both the core and complete joined smile must meet the existing 0.006 density margin.
  4. Run Calibrate All Expiries before publication. Every required expiry must have an accepted result and pass validation. Extrapolated months are seeded from accepted fits; failed rows leave the batch incomplete. Changes to the date, source, parameters, node edits, policy/code or saved base invalidate the corresponding batch evidence.
  5. For TTF, batch calibration targets settlement nodes, including applicable session node edits. The manual intraday workflow starts from the latest published smile when available. A manually adjusted candidate or modal Final is session state; it does not replace the complete batch required by the inline publication button. Review that distinction before using the TTF intraday adjustment guide. JKM uses its official COB path.
Policies: ttf_quote_preserving_core_wing_tail_hybrid_v2 and jkm_pchip_core_wing_tail_hybrid_v1. Check the final core, joins and tails together; an accepted Wing-only fit is insufficient.

Understand the acceptance gates

These are the reviewed policy defaults, not trader-tunable overrides. A volatility point means one percentage point of IV: 0.01 in decimal volatility. Shape checks apply to the continuous candidate as well as its sampled publication domain.

Confirm, publish and verify the selected revision

  1. Review the complete candidate and its source classes, forwards, expiries, fit errors and validation warnings. Resolve blocked or stale results by reloading and recalibrating the affected context.
  2. Confirm the complete batch, then use the product’s Validate & Publish action when its publication flag and your authenticated permission allow it. Brent rebuilds and compares the reviewed candidate; HH verifies its saved server-side candidate, code/output fingerprints, pinned settlement rows and expiry references. TTF/JKM verify complete batch evidence and rebuild the publication surface from the reviewed state. Active-base changes block replacement.
  3. Read the returned publication revision and persisted point count. Verify the active product/COB revision in the relevant consumer and inspect representative expiries. For Brent and HH, the Vol Trades overlay is exact-COB; for TTF/JKM, check the explicitly labelled publication COB rather than assuming it matches the market view.
  4. Keep the surface and portfolio publication steps separate: this activates a calibrated surface revision. Portfolio risk changes only after its own valuation draft, review and publication.

Exchange and OTC

Price with the correct contract and surface

The Pricer reads published references as well as accepting manual inputs. A product label alone is not enough to determine expiry, settlement, premium convention or sizing.

Pricing decisionWhat to check
Exchange vs OTCSelect the intended workspace and, for exchange products, the mapping ID. The registry carries the pricing model, premium convention, contract size, expiry convention and surface source.
Model and premium treatmentThe mapped model governs the calculation. Examples: ICE TTF and Brent use futures-style conventions; JKM average-price options use Asian-76. The Brent exchange label explicitly identifies Black-76 as American-equivalent futures-style. Do not apply a generic discount factor to every product.
Conditional mappingsSome mappings carry conditional evidence/status. The code labels ICE HH PHE premium evidence and JKM vanilla listing evidence as conditional; preserve those qualifications.
Surface referenceCheck publication ID/COB, source product, contract/option expiry, forward, interpolation or expiry adjustments, and any fallback/availability warning. Current inputs must match the returned surface reference before calculation.
Input volatility and adjustmentsWhere surface-driven volatility is used, inspect published volatility plus ATM, skew and smile adjustments. Manual overrides and custom expiries change the pricing scenario; they do not change the shared published surface.
Dates, quantities and unitsDistinguish valuation date, option expiry, contract/delivery dates and averaging period. Respect mapping-specific sizing, including TTF delivery hours and JKM monthly lots. Keep signed quantities and native currencies explicit.
Result scopeInspect all legs, total premium, payoff and sensitivities. Session results do not book the trade, persist a portfolio draft or publish official Greeks.

Desk routines

Six practical playbooks

Start of day

  1. Refresh Options Data and read the source-alignment banner.
  2. Compare portfolio COB with curves and operational surfaces; then separately check calibrated publication COB and revision.
  3. Inspect selected product/expiry coverage in Prices, Volatility Surface and Vol Trades.
  4. Open Greeks on the intended published COB and reconcile strategy/asset exposures.
  5. Use Trades for row-level marks, inputs and run lineage.
At this inspection, the portfolio lags the market tables by a month. A successful refresh does not close that gap.

Refresh and review a captured market

  1. In Vol Trades, select the product and inspect the captured snapshot’s kind, date and as-of time.
  2. If new data is needed, use the appropriate Bloomberg or settlement refresh control.
  3. Wait for worker completion and select/verify the resulting complete snapshot.
  4. Review quotes, trade prints, volume and open interest with their separate provenance.
  5. For JKM, use the official COB source path; for ICE Quotes, keep the Brent feed boundary visible.
Queue acceptance, worker readiness and a persisted complete snapshot are three separate checks.

Calibrate and publish a surface

  1. Select the intended market context, open Calibrate surface and check the locked input ledger.
  2. Run the product’s calibration action and review every required expiry, fit and validation warning.
  3. For Brent, review the single observed-strike SVI fit and the settlement/intraday diagnostics.
  4. When authorized and enabled, confirm the complete batch and use Validate & Publish.
  5. Read back the active publication revision and points, then refresh the relevant consumer.
A candidate, an exported workbook and a published surface are different states. Portfolio risk remains unchanged until its own valuation run is published.

Investigate a P&L move

  1. Open Valuation and select the intended COB, strategy and currency.
  2. Use the P&L Explain tab with ordered previous/current published COBs.
  3. Inspect changed prices, vols and correlations, as well as unchanged-mark/source warnings.
  4. Trace unexplained results to Trades and persisted model/input lineage.
  5. Keep TTF source diagnostics distinct from the official selected valuation inputs.
Do not add native-currency values into an unlabeled portfolio total. Unexplained P&L can contain higher-order and model effects.

Price and stress a trade idea

  1. Choose Exchange or OTC. For Exchange, verify the mapping, payoff model and premium convention.
  2. Check valuation date, option expiry, delivery/averaging dates, sizing and the published surface reference.
  3. Build the legs and inspect any manual volatility or forward adjustments.
  4. Calculate, then review premium, payoff, Greeks and source warnings.
  5. Use Scenarios for supported portfolio Kirk shocks; review excluded rows and currency scope.
The Pricer does not book a trade. Use the separate booking process before the next governed portfolio valuation.

End-of-day portfolio publication

  1. Confirm trades and exact-COB valuation inputs, including each product’s source policy and conventions.
  2. Run the governed backend’s dry valuation and review missing/mixed-input failures.
  3. Create an immutable draft and reconcile rows, currency totals, fingerprints and comparison evidence.
  4. Publish the approved run and verify the publication pointer/current-view readback.
  5. Refresh Greeks, Valuation and Trades and confirm the intended run and COB.
Surface publication does not replace the portfolio draft/review/publish sequence.

Interpretation and control

Trader guardrails

Keep the three dates distinct

Market COB, surface publication time and portfolio valuation COB serve different purposes. A newer timestamp does not prove newer underlying marks.

The banner is a partial freshness check

It checks global maxima for portfolio, from-prices surface and curve. It does not certify selected-product completeness, calibrated publication freshness or Bloomberg worker liveness.

Refresh, fit and publish have different effects

Top-bar refresh reads; Bloomberg refresh queues persistence; calibration creates a candidate; surface and portfolio publication activate different records.

Read flags by product

Generic writes can be off while Brent, TTF or JKM publication is on. Server-side identity, validation and explicit confirmation still apply.

Do not blend source policies

Brent uses its pinned snapshot; TTF/JKM use exact official inputs; HH uses complete LNE authority even when viewing ON.

Respect units and model scope

Use mapping-specific premium and quantity conventions. Keep native currencies explicit and inspect scenario exclusions.

An ICE quote is not an execution

The ICE Quotes section reads persisted values and status. Its display does not send an order or a chat response.

Changing context invalidates review

If the market snapshot, inputs or active base publication changes, reload/recalibrate and inspect the replacement candidate before publishing.

Read back what changed

Verify the actual published revision and consumer state. Health, a candidate preview or an exported file alone is not publication evidence.
